VLA DRONE STUDENT PACKAGE
Single VLA edition | 26 September 2026

YOUR PROJECT
Develop and evaluate one VLA that monitors a normal field survey, decides
when to detour, approaches visible litter, analyses and maps it, then resumes
unfinished coverage. It shares visual and language features across mission
decisions, candidate grounding, semantic labels and bounded movements.
Ordinary software preserves the route and validates commands; the existing
autopilot flies. No second VLM is required in the target system.

HOW TO USE THE EXAMPLES
Evaluation defaults to validation scenes. The updated workbook uses explicit
splits. Add --split test to the original guide's evaluation commands when
reproducing its reported test results.

The examples are reference outputs. Create your own results in new runs/
folders. Lab 3 regenerates the teaching data; the included copy also lets
you inspect its images and records immediately. The tiny-model checkpoint
is an educational NumPy policy, not pretrained SmolVLA. Use these results
to learn the workflow; do not present them as your own real-drone results.

WHAT NEEDS THE LAB
The laptop exercises have been executed. The PX4/Gazebo and SmolVLA scripts
still require their documented simulator/GPU smoke tests. Orin benchmarks,
the synchronised flight bridge and real flights require lab support and
validation. See VLA_Drone_Starter_Kit/docs/VALIDATION.md for the exact status.
The complete survey mission manager, unified model heads and their trainer
are research work to implement; the included SmolVLA trainer is movement-only.

Python libraries, the simulator and pretrained weights require installation
or downloads. The guide links to papers and official documentation; their
full texts and external software are not bundled. Training uses the lab
workstation; the Orin NX 16 GB is the proposed onboard inference computer.
